Background & Philosophy

Bridging the physical and computational realms through structured engineering discipline.

I am an undergraduate software and robotics engineer studying Information Technology at Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT Vellore).

My engineering philosophy is rooted in first-principles thinking: understanding hardware kinematics, mathematical solvers, and operating system mechanics from the ground up, rather than relying on black-box abstractions.

Whether configuring 200Hz PID velocity loops on microcontrollers, computing fluid approximations via Lattice Boltzmann equations, or architecting local-first agent runtimes, I prioritize clean interfaces, deterministic execution, and verifiable results.
