Background & Philosophy

Bridging mathematical solvers, embedded kinematics, and operating systems from first principles.

My engineering philosophy is rooted in first-principles thinking: understanding hardware kinematics, fluid dynamics, and operating system mechanics from the ground up, rather than relying on black-box abstractions.

I am an undergraduate software and robotics engineer studying Information Technology at Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT Vellore).

Whether configuring 200Hz closed-loop PID velocity regulation on ESP32 microcontrollers, computing fluid approximations via Lattice Boltzmann equations in WebGL, or architecting local-first agent execution engines, I prioritize clean interfaces, deterministic execution, and verifiable results.

Beyond standard web stacks, my focus centers on the intersection of physical machines and computational intelligence: high-performance systems programming, real-time telemetry, and hardware-interfaced software.
